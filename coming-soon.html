If Not Heaven

Forthcoming

By Ys Goldt
Edition details to be announced

An intimate historical romance

East Prussia, 1908

Father Markus Engel has knelt in the silence, telling himself that solitude is a virtue and that he does not deserve to be known.

Then, on a rain-soaked afternoon, he takes shelter in a wayside shrine and finds Philomena von Richthofen lighting candles for a God she does not believe in.

She asks the one question he cannot answer.

If Not Heaven is an intimate historical romance about two lonely people who learn to be known, and what it costs to finally let someone in.

A science-fantasy novel

The Vasentia were built to endure, to preserve, to remember, and to outlast humanity itself. They back up their memories and restore themselves from death, but anything lost between preservation and damage is gone forever.

For eleven hundred years, Eîra has lived alone among the ruins of a forgotten civilisation. Then Sâel arrives, and an unlikely meeting leaves him with two days missing from his memory and her with a truth she cannot bring herself to tell him.

How Long Things Hold is a story about memory, grief, and the fragile connections that give meaning to existence.
